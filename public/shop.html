HISTORICAL OVERVIEW

The Rothschild Family

The Rothschilds are a European banking family whose history began in 18th-century Frankfurt. During the 19th century, banking houses were established in several European centers. This section offers a source-based introduction and distinguishes documented history from myths and speculation.
